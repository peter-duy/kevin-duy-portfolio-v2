01 · How the experiment was built

The pipeline, end to end.

Every model below went through the same pipeline. Nothing here is a one-off run — the design is the point, and the test-set read-out at the end is the only scorecard published.

  1. STEP 01

    Student grades in

    Group project on student grades. The target: predict the grade outcome from earlier performance.

  2. STEP 02

    LASSO selection

    Feature selection with LASSO, glmnet — shrink the field before any model is fit.

  3. STEP 03

    70 / 30 split

    70% train, 30% held-out test. The test set stays untouched until the final read-out.

  4. STEP 04

    10-fold CV design

    Cross-validation designed into the project. No CV figures are published — see the honesty note below.

  5. STEP 05

    Four models fit

    Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, and LASSO evaluation — each by its credited builder.

  6. RESULT

    Test-set read-out

    Only the held-out test set is scored in public. That is the chart below — and nothing else.
